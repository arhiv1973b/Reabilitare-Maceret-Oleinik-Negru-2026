**Subject:** Restoration of Jus Cogens Integrity and Erga Omnes Obligations within the UN Charter Framework

### I. Diplomatic Preamble

This communication, under the legal seal A©tor / TI-ULA, is transmitted in recognition of the United States of America as the founding architect and constitutional guarantor of the United Nations Charter (San Francisco, 1945) and the Security Council structure.

Given the precedent-setting authority of the United States in the creation of the collective security mechanism and the ongoing relevance of Article 103 of the Charter, this memorandum reaffirms the universal obligations arising from *jus cogens* norms and their *erga omnes* implications — duties owed toward all members of the international community.

### II. Juridical Foundation

1. Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties (1969) – Article 53 and Article 64 establish that a peremptory norm (*jus cogens*) invalidates any treaty provision conflicting with it.

2. International Court of Justice, *Italy v. Germany* (2012) – clarifies the tension between sovereign immunity and *jus cogens* violations, confirming that violations of these norms transcend conventional state immunity.

3. Universal Character of Obligations (*erga omnes*) – as recognized in *Barcelona Traction* (ICJ, 1970), obligations such as the prohibition of aggression, genocide, slavery, and torture are owed to the international community as a whole.
