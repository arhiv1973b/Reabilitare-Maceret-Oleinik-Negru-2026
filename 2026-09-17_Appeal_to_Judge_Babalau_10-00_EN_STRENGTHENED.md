### Justification of the Key Value of the Decision (Determination of Judge Viorelia Grigoraș of 05.08.2024 in case No. 21-30/2021)

This procedural decision is fundamental and constitutes the essential basis of today’s appeal for the following reasons:

1. **Establishment of the procedural connection of the cases (Art. 42 CPC RM):** The Determination officially confirms the identity of the subject matter under examination (applications for judicial rehabilitation of Maceret Alexei) and the necessity of their joinder in order to ensure the correct administration of justice and to avoid separate and contradictory resolution of the cases.

2. **Fixation of jurisdictional priority:** The court recognised that the proceedings before Judge Tudor Stambol had been initiated earlier, thereby establishing its status as the primary instance for the joint examination of all related applications.

3. **Prevention of procedural abuse and fragmentation:** The decision blocks attempts at artificial division of the single subject matter of the dispute (rehabilitation) among different judges, creating a continuous chain of evidence and procedural acts (the cascade of cases Nos. 21-30 and 21-40).

Having examined in open court the application of petitioner Maceret Alexei for the transfer of the application for judicial rehabilitation lodged by Maceret Alexei pursuant to Art. 469 (1) point 5), Art. 470 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, for the examination of the question of its joinder with the application for judicial rehabilitation previously lodged by Maceret Alexei and pending before Judge Stambol Tudor,

**HAS ESTABLISHED:**

On 19 February 2021 the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) received the application for judicial rehabilitation lodged by Maceret Alexei pursuant to Art. 469 (1) point 5), Art. 470 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure. The said application was randomly assigned via the IIGD system on 19.02.2021 and was given registration number 21-30/2021.

On 24 July 2024, during the court hearing, petitioner Maceret Alexei lodged an application requesting that the said application for judicial rehabilitation be transferred to Judge Stambol Tudor as the previously seised instance, for examination of the possibility of its joinder with another application for judicial rehabilitation previously lodged by him, Maceret Alexei, and pending before Judge of the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) Stambol Tudor.

Petitioner Maceret Alexei maintained the lodged application at the hearing and requested that it be granted, since both applications have the same subject matter – judicial rehabilitation.

Defence counsel of the petitioner, lawyer Gonciar Vasile, supported the application of petitioner Maceret Alexei at the hearing, noting that another application for judicial rehabilitation lodged by Maceret Alexei earlier than the present application is pending before Judge of the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) Tudor Stambol (1-21009689-12-21-21012021), for the purpose of examining the possibility of their joinder for joint examination within a single set of proceedings.

Having heard the opinions of the participants in the proceedings and having studied the case materials, the court finds the application of petitioner Maceret Alexei well-founded and to be granted.

The following arguments are advanced in support of this conclusion.

Thus, it follows with certainty from the case materials that an application for judicial rehabilitation lodged by Maceret Alexei pursuant to Art. 469 (1) point 5), Art. 470 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure is pending before the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) and is being examined by Judge Grigoraș Viorelia.

At the same time, from the information presented by petitioner Maceret Alexei and his defence counsel, as well as from the data of the National Portal of Judicial Instances, it follows that an application for judicial rehabilitation lodged by Maceret Alexei (case No. 1-21009689-12-21-21012021) is pending before Judge Stambol Tudor of the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office), in which the court was seised earlier and which is already at the stage of judicial examination, the hearing having been fixed for 25.09.2024 at 09:00.

In accordance with the provisions of Art. 42 (1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, in the event of indivisibility or connection of criminal cases, the trial at first instance, if it takes place simultaneously with regard to all the facts and in respect of all co-perpetrators, is conducted by a single court.

Pursuant to Art. 42 (3) points 1), 2), 4) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, connection of criminal cases exists in the following cases: 1) when two or more offences are committed by different acts by one or more persons jointly, in the same place; 2) when two or more offences are committed by one person at different times or in different places; and 4) when there is a link between two or more offences and joinder of the cases is necessary for the correct administration of justice.

According to Art. 42 (4) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, in the event of joinder of cases concerning several persons accused of committing offences within the territorial competence of different courts of equal rank, or concerning one person accused of committing several offences, if those cases fall within the competence of two or more courts of equal rank, the proceedings are examined by the court that was seised earlier in the case.

Having regard to the fact that Judge of the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) Stambol Tudor was seised earlier and has begun examination of the application for judicial rehabilitation lodged by Maceret Alexei concerning the same judgment, for the purpose of a more rapid, complete and objective examination, and in order to avoid aggravation of the petitioner’s situation by separate resolution of the cases, on the basis of Art. 42 (4) of the Code of Criminal Procedure the court concludes that it is necessary to transfer to Judge of the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) Stambol Tudor the present application for judicial rehabilitation for resolution of the question of the possibility of its joinder with the application for judicial rehabilitation previously lodged by Maceret Alexei (1-21009689-12-21-21012021), as the court that was seised earlier.

In accordance with the provisions of Arts. 42, 43, 342, 348, 458, 460 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, the court

**ORDERS:**

The application of petitioner Maceret Alexei is granted and the application for judicial rehabilitation No. 21-30/2021; 1-21025885-12-21-19022021-1, lodged by Maceret Alexei pursuant to Art. 469 (1) point 5), Art. 470 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, is transferred to Judge of the Chișinău Court (Buiucani Office) Stambol Tudor for resolution of the question of joinder of the said application for judicial rehabilitation with the application for judicial rehabilitation previously lodged by Maceret Alexei (case No. 1-21009689-12-21-21012021), which is already pending and in which the court was seised earlier.

The Determination of 05 August 2024 is final.

### The link between the loss (or threat of destruction) of the materials of the key criminal case No. 1-568/98 of 13.10.1998 and the consequences at the international-law level (including the loss of support from the United States as a subject of international law and the deprivation of citizens of the rights under the UDHR and the ICCPR by virtue of the 1969 Vienna Convention) is determined by the following peremptory principles of public international law:

**Violation of the principle of good faith (Pacta sunt servanda) under the 1969 Vienna Convention**

Pursuant to Article 26 of the 1969 Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties, every treaty in force is binding upon the parties to it and must be performed by them in good faith.

The Republic of Moldova, by ratifying the international covenants on human rights, undertook the obligation to guarantee judicial protection and the right to rehabilitation.

The physical concealment, absence or preparation for disposal of the primary source of justice — criminal case No. 1-568/98 of 13.10.1998 — in the court archives constitutes performance of international obligations in bad faith (mala fides), destroying the very legal foundation of the State’s jurisdiction.

**Deprivation of the guarantees of the ICCPR and the UDHR (Right to an effective remedy)**

The Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR, Arts. 8, 10) and the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR, Arts. 2, 14) guarantee every person the right to a fair trial and to an effective remedy before a competent court.

The destruction or concealment of the basic procedural document (case 1-568/98), on which the entire chain of subsequent prosecutions and rehabilitation claims is built (including the cascade of cases before Judge Grigoraș and Judge Stambol), creates a situation of “denial of justice”.

Without the material carrier of the original judicial act, the realisation of rights under the ICCPR becomes legally impossible, because the State deprives the citizen of the objective subject matter of the dispute.

**Effect of Jus Cogens and State responsibility (ARSIWA)**

A gross violation of the right of access to justice and the destruction of archival evidence violate peremptory norms of international law (jus cogens), the obligations under which are erga omnes in character (owed to the international community as a whole).

According to the doctrine of State responsibility for internationally wrongful acts, the loss or destruction of judicial archives with the purpose of blocking the rehabilitation of a victim of unlawful prosecution qualifies as a systemic failure of the legal system.

**Loss of the legal and political grounds for support from the United States**

The interaction and support from the United States (within the framework of acts on the promotion of democracy, the rule of law and the fight against corruption, as well as standards of development assistance) are based on the strict observance by partner States of international conventions and human-rights standards.

When the judicial organs of the State reveal a practice of destroying key archival cases (from 1998) in circumvention of the established procedures for the protection of documents, this means the dismantling of the rule of law.

From the standpoint of international law, the loss of judicial documentation of this level breaks the legitimate chain of continuity of power and judicial decisions, which automatically deprives State institutions of the right to invoke the legitimacy of their own decisions before international partners and donors.
